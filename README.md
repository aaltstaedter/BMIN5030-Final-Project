### Project Description
Heat exposure is a serious public health issue. Heat exposure is also quite common
in the United States, especially in large cities such as New York City (NYC), where
extreme heat can exacerbate the heat island effect. Heat exposure is not only dangerous
for putting people at risk for experiencing heat-related illnesses (e.g., heat stroke),
but can also exacerbate people's pre-existing chronic conditions. Using a health equity lens,
this project seeks to explore the potential relationship between heat vulnerability and chronic condition prevalence 
in NYC. The data come from the NYC Department of Health and Mental Hygiene (NYC DOHMH) website and are publicly available for download. In addition to 
exploring relationships between heat vulnerability and chronic condition prevalence, this 
project also seeks to identify potential community-level disparities that warrant further consideration.

In this repository, the .qmd file that shows all of the code and analyses can be found.
